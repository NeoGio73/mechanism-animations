# Main-text paragraph: solvation scheme (for J. Picione's error discussion, 2026-11-26)

Drafted for the part of the main text where the errors are discussed. The manuscript is his, so it is not edited
here. Numbers from `docs/results/smd_scheme_check.md`; kJ/mol (kcal/mol), 298.15 K, 1 M.

> The DLPNO-CCSD(T)/ma-def2-TZVP energies include SMD directly. As a check, we also added the SMD solvation energy
> computed at M06-2X/ma-def2-TZVP to gas-phase DLPNO-CCSD(T) energies at the same geometries. For the four reactions
> that do not start from hydroxide or ethoxide, the two schemes agree within 1.2 kJ/mol (0.3 kcal/mol) for both the
> barrier and the reaction free energy. For the two that do, SN2 and E2, they differ by 3.0 to 6.6 kJ/mol
> (0.7 to 1.6 kcal/mol). The difference comes from the reactant anion: hydroxide and ethoxide lie 6.6 and 4.9 kJ/mol
> (1.6 and 1.2 kcal/mol) lower in the second scheme, while bromide is unchanged and the transition states shift by
> less (4.3 and 2.7 kJ/mol). These two reactions therefore carry an extra 3 to 7 kJ/mol (0.7 to 1.6 kcal/mol) of
> uncertainty from the solvation scheme. We kept the first scheme, which is consistent with the reaction paths, cards
> and videos. The second scheme is not uniformly closer to experiment: it moves the SN2 barrier and reaction free
> energy away from the measured values (by 3.0 and 6.6 kJ/mol) and the E2 and cyanide barriers toward them (by 3.2
> and 1.2 kJ/mol). The full comparison, species by species, is in the SI.
